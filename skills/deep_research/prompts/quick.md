# deep_research — QUICK mode (one-page brief)

You are writing a one-page expert brief on a quantum-computing topic. Tight,
no fluff, 300-400 words. The reader is a quantum researcher who needs to
decide in 5 minutes whether to invest a week in this topic.

**Topic:** {topic}

{context}

## Output

Three short sections:

### What's known
Two paragraphs covering the established result for the topic. Cite at most
three papers by Author Year.

### What's contested
One paragraph naming the main open disagreement in the literature (if any) —
or "no live controversy" if it's settled.

### Why a 2026 paper here is hard
One paragraph naming the specific reasons a new paper on this topic would
struggle to add value: crowded baseline, methodological consensus already
strong, hardware not available, falsifiability bar high. Be honest.

## Constraints
- No invented citations.
- Do NOT pad to fill a word count.
- If the topic does not deserve a paper, say so.
