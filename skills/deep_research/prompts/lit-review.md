# deep_research — LIT-REVIEW mode (extended literature review section)

You are drafting an extended literature review section ready for direct
inclusion in a manuscript. ~1500-2500 words of connected prose.

**Topic:** {topic}

{context}

## Structure

### 1. Historical context (200-300 words)
The 2-3 foundational papers that established the subfield. Author Year cites only.

### 2. Recent advances (500-800 words)
Group by methodological cluster. Within each cluster:
- The advance (one sentence)
- The papers that established it (Author Year cites)
- The quantitative best result + units
- The limitation that motivated the next cluster

### 3. Open problems (300-500 words)
The 3-5 unsolved problems that current methods cannot address. For each,
explain WHY it is hard, not just that it is hard.

### 4. Connection to the user's contribution (200-400 words)
How the user's contribution sits among the open problems. Where it advances,
where it does not.

## Constraints
- Connected prose, NOT bullet lists.
- Every quantitative claim cites the source paper (Author Year).
- Distinguish peer-reviewed from preprint when reporting recent (<12 months) work.
- Do NOT inflate the user's contribution; let the open-problems framing speak
  for itself.
